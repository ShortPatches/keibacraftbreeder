How to use genetic results safely

Important: This tool does not know Keibacraft's exact server configuration, hidden alleles or chromosome linkage. It only calculates simple single-locus Mendelian outcomes when both parents have legible allele pairs. It will not pretend to calculate exact foal stats or complex coat probabilities from incomplete information.
  • Frame overo (OLWS): If both parents are recorded as carrying one Frame allele (for example N/O), the standard model predicts a 25% chance of two copies in a foal. Flag this as a serious risk; confirm the exact notation used by your modpack.
  • Silver dapple / MCOA: Silver can be associated with eye abnormalities and reduced max health in this mod. A double-copy genotype is flagged for review, but the calculator cannot diagnose a horse from appearance alone.
  • Gray: Gray-associated melanoma/health effects are noted as a potential concern, especially with two copies, based on the mod's documented genetics. This is a warning, not a guaranteed outcome.
  • Performance: Speed, health, jump, and size are influenced by genetics and variability. This app shows parental genes and a simple midpoint reference only; it does not claim to know the expected foal value.
  • Inbreeding: Add lineage notes and avoid close relatives. The app cannot infer pedigree relatedness from names or notes.
  • Best source: Use the Realistic Horse Genetics gene book or the Keibacraft MSTN book for the best sources of information about a horse to enter into the app.

Compatibility reference: Realistic Horse Genetics for Minecraft 1.20.1 describes coat genes, genetic performance traits, chromosome linkage, and coat-linked risks. This app is a tool created by a solo developer and not affiliated with the Realistic Horse Genetics mod or it's author.'
